Jess is a [[Class]] 3 [[Chromatics|Chromatic]]. She wields two short blades.

Occasionally is sent on missions with the [[Card Chromatics]].

Prior to both the [[Starting Point]] and becoming a Chromatic, she was good friends with [[Dev]], and once they discover that they are both Chromatics, they secretly meet together in various spots to keep in touch with each other.
