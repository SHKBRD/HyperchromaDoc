Cole is a [[Class]] 2 Pink [[Chromatics|Chromatic]].

# Personality

Cole typically doesn't interact with others in his daily activities, though he's good at being able to tell if the people around him need assistance, and tries to help out if he is able to. He is somewhat desensitized to violence and extreme emotions as a result of his time as a mercenary, though he holds a high level of sympathy for those who do experience those emotions.

# Character Backstory

Cole was involved in a well-known [[Mercenaries]] group, and eventually turned into a right-hand man for one of the organization's leads. After instability broke out across the organization as a result of constant targeting from the Chromatics, Cole quickly became disillusioned with the organization, as slowly started to lose the trust and abilities granted to him by other members of the organization. As a result, he decided to become a Chromatic to help regain a sense of authority over his actions in some other area of the criminal world. 

One of Cole's additional conditions to become a Chromatic included providing the Chromatics with all of the intelligence he had pertaining to his former organization, along with assisting in missions that targeted the organization. Among the missions he currently takes on, he still finds himself encountering the organization regularly, and he continues to fight against their remaining members.
# Character Relationships
## [[Claire]]
Cole has heard of Claire as one of the stronger Chromatics in his [[Chromatic Station|station]]. Cole knows Claire is a controversial figure among other members in the station, and he tries to avoid places where he thinks Claire may be around to prevent any interaction with her.
