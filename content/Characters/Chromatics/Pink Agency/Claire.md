# Personality

Claire is a very self-centered person, often unafraid of insulting people to their face. She is very confident in performing her missions alone, and is very sensitive in regards to people doubting her ability to achieve her goals. She holds strong grudges against people that appear to be weaker than her, enough to actively ignore them when prompted to interact or collaborate with them.
