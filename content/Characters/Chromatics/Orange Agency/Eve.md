Eve is a [[Class]] 5 [[Chromatics|Chromatic]].

She is often found together with [[Dawn]], her identical twin sister, who is also a Chromatic. She typically wears black, in contrast to her sister, who typically wears white.

# Personality

Eve usually keeps to herself, and is typically pessimistic about the life around her. She thoroughly thinks through her goals, though she is often scared to commit to them once the opportunity comes to act on the goal. She avoids confrontation or interaction with other by default, and it often takes an extended amount of time for her to gain a closer relationship with any given person.

# Character Relationships
## [[Dawn]]
Eve's identical twin sister. They typically perform/partake in many activities together, simultaneously. This typically includes missions, recreational activities, hangouts, though they can be found separated when interacting with characters that have differing perceptions of the twins.

Eve typically has to argue against Dawn's plans and ideas, and usually either gives up on listening to dawn, or caves in to satisfy Dawn. Eve doesn't really get how Dawn can interact with so many other people comfortable, though she has come to understand that it likely makes Dawn happy to know and talk with so many other people.

She often confines her more personal feelings into Dawn, occasionally setting aside time to communicate her feelings, helping Eve express her emotions.

## [[Dusk]]
Eve's older brother. Eve holds respect for Dusk, as she felt like he was an example of an honest person that she could rely on when it came to making promises and dealing with various serious situations. Eve misses Dusk dearly, and is worried that Dusk would think badly of her and Dawn if he found out that they have become Chromatics, especially worrying that he may see the conversion as some form of betrayal.

## [[Kade]]
Eve knows about Kade from [[Dawn]]'s relationship with him. Eve senses that Kade's outward feelings and intentions are disingenuous, which includes his relationship with Dawn, to his self-confident image. She suspects that Kade is trying to take advantage of Dawn to fulfill a materialistic desire for a relationship.
