---
title: Scenarios
---

All Scenarios involving the Chromatics universe occur after the [[Starting Point]], and are not considered "Canon" in terms of any established story. Scenarios may take place any time after the Starting Point, and generally follow along a story that includes a select group of [characters](#Character). This choice is made from the fact that I am very indecisive when it comes to creating a long term defined plot and story, especially with a cast of characters that are all supposed to kill each other. "When do I kill off this character?" and "Do I have to focus on this character right now?" are both questions that I don't need to definitively answer across my entire character roster, and I can write pretty much anything that lines up with what the characters in the story would do, in any scenario I choose!

This also allows for other people to come up with their own scenarios pretty freely, because no scenario is necessarily "Canon" anyway. The only "canon" restrictions that I follow are defined with [[Past Events|the events that happen before the Starting Point]]. Anyone can make up their own scenarios, and those scenarios are going to be just as canon as mine.
