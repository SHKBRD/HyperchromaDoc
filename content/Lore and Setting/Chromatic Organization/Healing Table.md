Healing tables are the most prominent and effective way to completely heal a [[Chromatics|Chromatic]] of their wounds and ailments. They are only used in [[Chromatic Station|Chromatic stations]], and use a larger supply of the [[Serums|Healing Serum]] to cover any injuries that a Chromatic might have.

Healing tables are used in the Medical wing of a Chromatic station, and each table is placed in its own room. Each table can be remotely or physically monitored by the medical staff in the station to ensure that the Chromatic is safely healing.

Each table is large enough to support an 8 foot tall person, and is fitted with equipment that monitors the Chromatic's wounds, conditions, ailments, infections and fractures, as well as equipment to help with the healing process. This includes mechanical arms that can administer IVs and move the Chromatic's limbs and clothes on the table, along with sanitation jets to clean the Chromatic off of any blood or environmental dirt, and driers to prepare the Chromatic to leave the table cleanly.

When in use, the table is closed with a glass lid to closely monitor the Chromatic's oxygen intake.

The process of using a healing table is as follows.

First, the Chromatic either makes an appointment to reserve a healing table, or enters an available healing table room if they are either entering or returning from the public.

If the Chromatic is wounded, the Chromatic should remove any clothing that directly covers/surrounds their wounds, though if removing the article of clothing would damage/amplify a wound further, the Chromatic can still use the table with the staff manually overseeing more of the table's procedures. Clothes can also be left to be patched and cleaned by a staff tailor during the time that the Chromatic is on the table.

The Chromatic then lays down onto the table with their back against the table's surface, and their legs and arms slightly spread out to give room for any remote injections. Once on the table, either the Chromatic or a staff member punctures the user with a needle to administer both the initial [[Serums#Healing Serum|Healing Serum]] and an anesthetic.

From there, the Chromatic will remain unconscious through the rest of their time on the table. Whatever wounds, fractures or infections are present are then sanitized and treated with the healing serum, and any debris lodged in the body is also removed. If the Chromatic previously opted into keeping the scars of their wounds, scars will not be treated. Whatever blood was lost is replaced, with any new blood [[Blood Encoding|encoded]] to the treated Chromatic.

Once the Chromatic is fully healed and is cleaned of any blood and debris, they are no longer provided anesthesia, and are then woken up to collect their belongings and leave the healing table's room.
