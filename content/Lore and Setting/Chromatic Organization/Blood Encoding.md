Blood encoding is the process of adding computer readable data to the blood cells of a [[Chromatics|Chromatic]].

The main purpose of blood encoding is to add unique identification data to a Chromatic that does not involve reading a Chromatic's DNA. This is used in tandem with [[Mesh Metal|mesh metal]] blades, which stores liquids that come in contact with its surface.

For example, If a Chromatic wounds and kills an opposing Chromatic with a blade that uses mesh metal, the opposing Chromatic's blood can then be taken to a [[Weapon Rinsing|weapon rinsing site]] to read its encoded data and reward the winning Chromatic depending on the Class rank stored in the encoded blood.

Any changes to a single encoded blood cell's data will modify any surrounding encoded blood cells near instantaneously, as long as that blood cell is inside of that Chromatic's body. Once an encoded blood cell leaves a Chromatic's body, it cannot be modified any further.

Below are the types of tracked data that encoded blood stores.

- The Chromatic's identification number (which can then be used to look up their [[Agency]] and [[Class]] rank)
- The Chromatic's Consciousness (Conscious, Unconscious)
- The Chromatic's Vitality (Alive, Dead)
- The identification number of any mesh metal blade that comes in contact with the cell (for tracking which opposing Chromatics damaged the receiving Chromatic, see [[Class|Class credits]])

Roughly 1/5th of a Chromatic's blood is encoded, and any lost blood through their body's internal regulations or wounds are replaced upon visiting a [[Healing Table|healing table]].
