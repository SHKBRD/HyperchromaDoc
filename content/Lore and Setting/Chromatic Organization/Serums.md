These are the serums that [[Chromatics|Chromatics]] can use.

- [[Serums#Transformation Serum]]
- [[Serums#Healing Serum]]

Serums are made to cater to a specific agency's color, and serve various purposes for the Chromatics.

# Transformation Serum

Transforms the injected person into a Chromatic of a specific agency's color. Initiates [[Blood Encoding|blood encoding]], provides higher pain tolerance, higher endurance, heightened senses, significantly slower metabolism, and a reduced need for sleep/rest.

# Healing Serum

Regenerates damaged body parts. As the serum flows through the Chromatic's bloodstream, it seeks the nearest damaged/open wounds and uses its own molecules to generate new cells in place of the damaged/torn cells. This heals wounds entirely, prevents bleeding, and it relieves pain fairly quickly once the damaged cells are regenerated.

This serum does not recreate severed body parts, but the serum does try its best to heal the exposed flesh as cleanly as possible. For example, if the serum entered the bloodstream of an arm that is severed of its hand, it would round off the arm's stump, generating flesh that covers the wound, and would repair the nearest nerve routes to alleviate any disorientation that comes with not having a hand anymore.

Healing Serum also makes its user feel more exhausted, since the user's body is tricked into thinking that it is performing the cell generation by itself, and begins to signal signs of tiredness the administered area.

Chromatics are given syringes of the Healing Serum to use on the field, though using them _during_ combat is discouraged due to the tiredness that comes soon after administering the serum.
