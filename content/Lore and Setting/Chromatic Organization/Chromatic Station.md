# Housing/Living

There are various bedrooms and personal spaces that Chromatics can rent out using their [[Points]]. These rooms can be occupied with any amount of people at a time, and the rooms generally serve to give private time between any Chromatics who stay in them. Chromatics can also rent the bedrooms if they want to sleep at all. Because of the reduced need for rest due to the [[Serums#Initial Serum|Initial Serum]] taken by all Chromatics, Chromatics usually take some kind of drug that aids with falling asleep, which is typically provided with the room.

# Dining/Food

All Chromatic stations have a dining area/cafeteria where Chromatics can redeem their [[Points]] for food (Chromatics aren't necessarily required to eat in the first place, read [[Serums#Initial Serum|Initial Serum]] for more.) All food is made in the station, and each station has a variety of food typically related to the local cuisine. Because of their slower metabolism and generally reduced need to eat food due to the [[Serums#Initial Serum|Initial Serum]] taken by all Chromatics, Chromatics usually take a drug to mitigate their body's reaction to the food being eaten as to not produce too much energy. After eating their food or before going to a mission, Chromatics can then visit a [[Healing Table]] to clear their body of the drug and any remaining food in their body.

# Healing/Recovery services

This is mostly relegated to the [[Healing Table|Healing Tables]] and the professionals who run them. Healing services are available near the entrances of stations, as well as the various training areas around each station.
