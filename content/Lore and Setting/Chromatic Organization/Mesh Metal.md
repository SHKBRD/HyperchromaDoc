This type of metal is the primary metal used in [[Weapons|Chromatic weapons]].
This material consists of a strong web of other metals, leaving extremely small gaps for liquids to seep through. The web is made of thin films of other metals forming a grid of small hexagonal slits, which lightly overlap onto the slits of other metal films. The material is somehow quite firm and stable, though it is not very malleable, since it would close whatever gaps that are bent fairly easily. Because of this, most shapes need to be cut rather than molded, and sharpening blades that use mesh metal can only be realistically done in [[Chromatic Station|Chromatic stations]].

Chromatic blades use Mesh Metal to confirm opposing [[Chromatics]] kills via storing Chromatic blood and verifying the [[Blood Encoding|blood encoding]] on the stored blood. Mesh metal also interacts with the encoded blood itself, storing values like the amount of force the blade pushes against a target's flesh and identification details of both the blade and the Chromatic that inflicted damage upon the receiving Chromatic. The damaged Chromatic's blood is then stored elsewhere in the weapon after coming in contact with the blade, and it can then be extracted via a[[Weapon Rinsing| weapon rinse]] to redeem any credit earned from the kill.
