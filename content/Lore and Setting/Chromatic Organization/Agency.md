The [[Chromatics|Chromatic]] agencies are small sections of the [[Chromatic Organization]] that act as their own branch, and each agency competes with the others in order to receive the best organization evaluations. Higher evaluations net better perks for the Chromatics under that agency, and Chromatics are encouraged to both complete missions and kill opposing Chromatics in order to boost their agency evaluations.

Each Chromatic agency has multiple [[Chromatic Station|stations]] across the globe to house, aid and brief their Chromatics.

There are 6 agencies, each corresponding to one color.
- Red
- Blue
- Green
- Purple
- Pink
- Orange

Other agency colors do not exist, as each agency is supposed to have distinct colors. Chromatics will also bleed the color of their agency, so additional colors like yellow or brown would make lethal fights with Chromatics look more gross than they need to. These colors are also supposed to have full saturation and value, so only full hues would be considered as possible agency colors.
