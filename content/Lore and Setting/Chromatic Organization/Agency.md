The [[Chromatics|Chromatic]] agencies are small branches of the [[Chromatic Organization]], and each agency competes with one another to receive the best organization evaluations. Higher evaluations net better perks for the Chromatics under that agency, and Chromatics are encouraged to both complete missions and kill opposing Chromatics in order to boost their agency's evaluations.

Each Chromatic agency has multiple [[Chromatic Station|stations]] across the globe to house, aid and brief their Chromatics on upcoming missions.

There are 6 agencies, each corresponding to one color.

- Red
- Blue
- Green
- Purple
- Pink
- Orange
