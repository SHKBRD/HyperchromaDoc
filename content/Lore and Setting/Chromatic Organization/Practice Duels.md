[[Chromatics]] typically train themselves by dueling other Chromatics in [[Chromatic Station|Agency Stations]]. These usually involve fighting with each Chromatic's own lethal weapons and take place in the underground city simulation areas accessible from an Agency's station. Chromatics also occasionally form informal bets on who may win in a practice duel, either on themselves or other Chromatics.

Chromatics usually duel in three ways:

| Most common name for duel type | Description of duel                                                              |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First Strike                   | Duel lasts until an opponent is about to land the first strike of the fight      |
| (#) Strikes                    | Duel lasts until an opponent has landed a set number of strikes                  |
| Killing Strike                 | Duel lasts until an opponent is about to land a strike that kills their opponent |
