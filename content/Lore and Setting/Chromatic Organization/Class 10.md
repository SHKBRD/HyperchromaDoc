Rather than being a direct continuation of the 9th [[Class]] rank, Class 10 acts more as a coveted position across every [[Chromatics|Chromatic]]. Only one Chromatic across all six [[Agency|agencies]] may be awarded the Class 10 rank at a time. Once a Class 10 is killed by another Chromatic, That Chromatic is awarded with the Class 10 rank.

If a Class 10 Chromatic is killed by something other than an opposing Chromatic, the Chromatic with the highest Class rank and credits amongst all agencies is then awarded the Class 10 rank.
