Chromatics are highly skilled bio-enhanced assassins that complete contracts for the [[Chromatic Organization]]. Once someone is qualified to become a Chromatic, they are injected with a [[Serums|Serum]] that provides them with stronger athletic capabilities. The same serum also transforms the Chromatic's blood to take on the [[Chroma]] color of their assigned agency. Features like their hair and irises also take on that color, distinguishing themselves from non-Chromatics.

All Chromatics must wear a form of face covering (like a mask, visor, etc.) during missions to avoid being recognized in the public ~~(and it helps up their style points!)~~

Chromatics are also given a [[Class|Class Rank]] that indicates their experience as a Chromatic, determines the payouts that the Chromatic receives, and contributes to their agency's overall score. Gaining a higher Class Rank requires a Chromatic to kill other Chromatics from opposing agencies to prove their combat expertise.

In order to confirm missions and opposing Chromatic kills, Chromatics also wield bladed weapons made with a [[Mesh Metal|synthesized metal]] specifically designed to store any blood that touches its surface. That blood can later be traced back to whoever was injured/killed with it, and the Chromatic can then be attributed with their appropriate payouts for killing particular targets/Chromatics.
