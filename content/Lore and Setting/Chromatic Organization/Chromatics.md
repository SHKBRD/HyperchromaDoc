Chromatics are highly skilled assassins that complete contracts. Once someone is qualified to become a Chromatic, they are injected with a [[Serums|Serum]] that provides them with stronger athletic capabilities. The same serum also transforms the Chromatic's blood to take on the color of their agency. Features like their hair and irises also take on the color, distinguishing themselves from non-Chromatics.

All Chromatics must have a type of face covering (like a mask, visor, etc.), though it's only because it looks cool, and not for any stealthy/identity reasons.

Chromatics are assigned to their own [[Agency]], which determines which color their design is based on. They are also given a [[Class|Class Rank]] that indicates their experience as a Chromatic. Gaining a higher Class requires a Chromatic to kill other Chromatics from opposing agencies to prove their combat expertise. 

In order to confirm missions and opposing Chromatic kills, Chromatics also wield bladed weapons made with a [[Mesh Metal|synthesized metal]] specifically designed to keep track of whatever blood touches its surface. That blood can later be traced back to whoever was injured/killed with it, and the Chromatic can then be attributed with their appropriate payouts.
